Misha Money privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026 · አማርኛ

Summary

Misha Money is a personal finance tracker. Everything it records stays on your phone. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and it never uploads your messages or transactions.

SMS messages

With your permission, Misha reads text messages from banks and wallets it recognises (for example CBE, telebirr, Awash, Dashen, Bank of Abyssinia and Zemen) to record transactions automatically. Messages from other senders are skipped and never stored. Bank messages are processed on the phone; the original text of each transaction is kept so you can check it. Misha never sends SMS.

What is stored

Transactions, accounts, loans, budgets, categories and your settings, in a database on the phone that is encrypted with a key held in the Android keystore. Your app-lock PIN is stored only as a salted hash, also in the keystore. Fingerprints are handled by Android; Misha never sees them.

Sharing

Misha shares nothing on its own. Data leaves the phone only when you export a backup, a CSV file or a crash log and choose where to send it. Exported files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe. Automatic Android cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Notifications

Daily summaries and budget alerts are created on the phone. Nothing is sent through a push server.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase all data deletes everything. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data.
